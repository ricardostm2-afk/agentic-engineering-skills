Phase 2 — Project interview

Select for an unresolved product/architecture decision or requested planning. Existing/small repo
preparation normally infers what it needs and skips this interview. Resolve third-party methods
through `references/host-compatibility.md`; they are optional.

Read `references/project-grill.md` (question bank by dimension, order and stopping criterion).
If `mattpocock-skills:grilling` is available, invoke it with the Skill tool and supply phase 0's
context; keep the question rules above (the reference explains how boundary rounds and up to
4 questions per call fit together). For domain vocabulary, invoke
`mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling` (or apply the method in the reference).

Output when needed: lasting decisions (who decided, why, reversible?) and scope in the existing
decision location or `docs/decisions.md`; a glossary only for domain terms needing shared names.

Done when the selected work's material questions are resolved or delegated, and no open decision
would change its scope or architecture. Unrelated dimensions do not become interview obligations.
